## [0.1.0](https://github.com/isrockfeel/moonwitness/releases/tag/v0.1.0) (2026-11-11)

### Baseline

- Establishes the engineering foundation: `dev`/`main`/`master` branch topology, local gate engine
  (`gate:quick`, `gate:local`, `gate:push`, `gate:release`, `gate:doctor`), Conventional Commits,
  Release Please, and the CI/security workflows.
- `main` and `master` carry the same history as `dev` at this point; this entry is the SemVer
  baseline, not a feature release.
